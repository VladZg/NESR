## Mission statement

Provide vessels and maritime operators along the Northern Sea Route with continuous communication and timely, all-weather sea-ice information, enabling safe, efficient and year-round Arctic navigation.

## Need

Ships and the people who route them through ice decide with ice information that is old, incomplete or cannot reach the ship: there is almost no terrestrial infrastructure, geostationary satellites are low on the horizon above about 75°N, optical sensors fail in cloud and polar night, and ice moves within hours.

## Goals, objectives and measures of effectiveness

| Goal | Objective | MOE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 Continuous connectivity | Two-way data link to ships at 66–82°N | Availability ≥ 99.0% (TBR) in the service area (route ±20 nm and ports); delivery within the time of the message class (REQ-003 TR-35) |
| G2 All-weather ice observation | Image NSR ice independent of cloud and daylight | Ice chart resolution ≤ 50 m; information age ≤ 24 h (TBR) |
| G3 Timely delivery | Ice products and warnings reach the bridge in time to act | Warning lead time ≥ 6 h in ≥ 90% of events |
| G4 Maritime awareness | Live picture of all connected ships for authorities and owners | Position age ≤ 30 min |
| G5 Emergency support | Distress information to Rescue Services | Rescue request ≤ 2 min after the distress alert, with time-stamped last known position and uncertainty radius; supplements GMDSS |
