# Stakeholder register

| CI | Version | Status | Owner | Updated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-002 | v0.1 | Draft | Jash Joshi | 2026-10-29 |

| Code | Stakeholder | Role in the system | Main concern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SO | Ship operators (master and crew) | Send ship data, receive route updates and warnings | Safe passage through ice, day or night, in any weather |
| OW | Ship and cargo owners | Receive voyage status and route decisions | Schedule, fuel cost, cargo safety, data confidentiality |
| LO | Logistics Operators | Plan and follow voyages on the mission dashboard | Predictable ETAs, early warning of delays |
| PA | Port Authorities | Issue navigation and safety directives, watch traffic | Safety and rule compliance on the whole route |
| DI | Dispatcher | Approves route changes and sends commands to ships | Clear, trustworthy information |
| RS | Rescue Services | Receive rescue requests, report resource availability | Fast, accurate distress data |
| SS | Satellite Systems' Operators | Provide SAR, meteo, relay and navigation services | Requests within capacity, clear interfaces |
| RG | Regulators (frequency authority, classification society) | Set rules the system must follow | Compliance with radio and marine equipment rules |
| MS | Maintenance staff (satellite and ship) | Keep terminals and ground equipment working | Fast fault finding and repair |

The Data Analysis Centers are treated as part of the system. Stakeholder requirements (SR) are in [REQ-003](T3_REQ-003_System-and-Technical-Requirements.md).
